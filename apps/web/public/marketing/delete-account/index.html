Delete your SlyTab account

App: SlyTab (ca.electricrv.slytab) · Last updated: July 27, 2026

You can delete your SlyTab account and personal data at any time. There are two ways to do it — one inside the app, one by email if you can't sign in.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open SlyTab and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account…
  3. Type your email address to confirm, then tap Delete forever.

This takes effect straight away. It works the same in the web app at electricrv.ca/slytab.

By email (if you can't sign in)

Email dave@drscapital.com from the address on the account, with the subject SlyTab account deletion request. We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your email address, display name and avatar.
  • Your password and any Google or Apple sign-in link.
  • Your payment handles (Interac e-Transfer email, PayPal.Me, Venmo).
  • Every session — you're signed out everywhere immediately.
  • Your push notification tokens, so the app can no longer reach your devices.
  • Your group memberships: you leave every group.

What stays, and why

Expenses you shared with other people are not erased, because they are also their records — deleting them would silently change what your friends owe each other. Those entries remain in the group with your name replaced by “Deleted user”, so the maths still adds up but nothing identifies you.

If you want an expense removed as well as your account, delete it in the app first, or ask another member of the group to. Receipt photos you uploaded belong to the expense and follow the same rule.

Backups

Nightly database backups are kept for 30 days, so a copy of your data can persist in those for up to a month before ageing out. They are only ever used to restore the service after a failure.
