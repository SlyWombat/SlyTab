SlyTab support

Electric RV · Ontario, Canada

Email us: info@electricrv.ca

We usually reply within a couple of days. If you are reporting a problem, the fastest route is in the app: Profile → Report a bug. That attaches your app version and a screenshot, which saves a round of questions, and you will get an email when it is fixed.

Common questions

Someone added an expense and the split looks wrong. Open the expense and check the split method — Equal, Exact, Shares or percentage. Anyone in the group can edit an expense, and deleting one can be undone straight afterwards from the Expenses tab.

Amounts in a currency I don't use. A group has its own home currency, and each expense keeps the currency it was entered in. Under each amount you will also see the value in your own currency at today's rate. Set yours in Profile → Default currency.

Receipt scanning got the total wrong. Tap the amount and correct it — the scan is a starting point, not the final word. Some currencies use no decimal places, and a receipt printed as 88.930 means 88,930, not 88.93. If it reads a receipt badly, please report it with the photo attached so we can add it to our test set.

I want to leave a group. Ask any member to remove you, or email us and we will do it. A self-service option is on the way.

Your data

Receipt photos are parsed on our own server — they are not sent to any third-party AI service, and location metadata is removed when they arrive. See the privacy policy for the detail.

You can delete your account and data yourself: Profile → Delete my account… in the app, or see how account deletion works.
