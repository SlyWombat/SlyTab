What it does

The whole job, in the order you actually do it.

Scan the receipt

Photograph it and the total, the date and the line items come back filled in. Split by item when three of you shared the starter and one didn't.

Split it fairly

Equally, by exact amounts, by shares, by percentage, or equally after someone's +/− adjustment. Whoever paid, however many paid.

See where you stand

One balance per person across every group, in your own currency, with the original amount always shown next to it.

Settle up

Record a payment, the other person confirms it, and the balance clears. Interac, PayPal.Me and Venmo handles live on each profile.

Travel without maths

Enter an expense in the currency you paid in. It converts at that day's rate and the rate is kept, so the number never changes underneath you.

Bring your history

Import from Splitwise, mapping their members onto people you already know. Duplicates are dropped rather than doubled.

Questions people actually ask

Fuller answers live in the manual and the knowledge base.

Does everyone need the app?

No. It runs in any browser, and you can add someone by email before they have signed up at all — their share is tracked from the moment you add them.

What happens with several currencies in one group?

Each expense keeps the currency it was paid in. The group has a home currency, and anything foreign is converted at the reference rate for the date of the expense — then that rate is stored, so the balance cannot drift afterwards. You can override the rate if your card charged something different.

Where do my receipt photos go?

To our own server, which runs the model that reads them. They are not sent to any third-party AI service. Location metadata is removed when the photo arrives, so a receipt cannot reveal where you were.

Someone paid me back in cash. Now what?

Record it as a settlement. The other person gets asked to confirm it, and once they do the balance between you clears.

Can I get my data out?

Yes — export any group's full history as CSV from the group screen on the web app.

How do I leave a group?

Open the group, tap its name, then Leave this group. You will be asked to settle up first if you still owe or are owed — past expenses stay so nobody else's balance changes.
