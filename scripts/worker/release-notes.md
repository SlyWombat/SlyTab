The iPhone version of SlyTab has just been updated.

Install or update it here: {{TESTFLIGHT_URL}}

Open that on your iPhone. If you do not have TestFlight yet, the link will
send you to install it first, then bring you back to SlyTab.

What is new:

  - You can set a photo of yourself instead of the coloured initial. Profile
    -> Add a photo. It shows wherever your name appears, and only to people
    you share a group with.
  - Photos taken in portrait no longer come out sideways.
  - Replacing your photo now shows the new one straight away, rather than the
    old one until the app forgot it.
  - Adding an expense: the keyboard now gets out of the way when you scroll,
    instead of sitting over the split controls and the save button.
  - SlyTab will now tell you when a newer version is out, rather than leaving
    you to find out. There is a link to get it, and you can dismiss it.
  - If someone you know runs their own copy of SlyTab, the app can now connect
    to it. Profile -> Add another server. Each server keeps its own account and
    its own sign-in, and the app always asks before it moves.

Carried over from the update before it, in case you skipped that one:

  - When a receipt has no total we can find — a card statement, a faded till
    roll — we add up the lines we did read and fill that in.
  - Groups open faster, and one you have opened before appears straight away
    while the current figures load behind it.

If anything is wrong, please tell us from Profile -> Report a bug.
