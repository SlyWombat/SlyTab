The iPhone version of SlyTab has just been updated to 1.2.

Install or update it here: {{TESTFLIGHT_URL}}

Open that on your iPhone. If you do not have TestFlight yet, the link will
send you to install it first, then bring you back to SlyTab.

What is new since the last one:

  - You can now settle up from either end. Until now only the person who
    OWED money could record a payment, which meant whoever fronted the cash
    on a trip had no way to close a balance. Tap anyone's balance and record
    what they handed you — including part of it, because "here is twenty
    dollars towards my tab" is how money actually moves.
  - A trip can be locked for settling up when the spending is over. A locked
    group takes no new expenses, so the numbers hold still while everyone
    pays each other. Payments and reminders carry on, and anyone can unlock
    it when the receipt somebody forgot turns up.
  - Once a trip is locked, you can send one reminder to someone who still
    owes you. It will not nag: one email, not again for days, and never to
    someone who has turned SlyTab emails off.

Carried over from the update before it, in case you skipped that one:

  - You can set a profile photo instead of the initials badge, and choose
    the crop.
  - When a receipt has no total we can find, we add up the lines we did read
    and fill that in, with the explanation against the amount.

If anything is wrong, please tell us from Profile -> Report a bug.
