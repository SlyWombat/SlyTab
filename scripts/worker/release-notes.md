The iPhone version of SlyTab has just been updated.

Install or update it here: {{TESTFLIGHT_URL}}

Open that on your iPhone. If you do not have TestFlight yet, the link will
send you to install it first, then bring you back to SlyTab.

The main thing in this update:

  - Notifications now work. They never have. The app was failing to register
    for them silently, so the switch in your profile was promising something
    that could not happen. If you turned notifications on and wondered why
    nothing ever arrived, that was our bug, not your phone.

Also new:

  - An invite link now opens the app directly instead of bouncing you to the
    website and asking you to sign in again.
  - A light theme, and a System / Dark / Light control in your profile. It
    follows your phone's setting by default.
  - A proper icon set throughout, replacing the emoji.
  - "My expenses" in the Activity tab: everything you have entered, in one
    list, sortable by date or amount.
  - A user guide inside the app, under Profile, if you want to know how
    something works.

If anything is wrong, please tell us from Profile -> Report a bug.
