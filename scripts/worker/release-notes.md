The iPhone version of SlyTab has just been updated to 1.2.1.

Install or update it here: {{TESTFLIGHT_URL}}

Open that on your iPhone. If you do not have TestFlight yet, the link will
send you to install it first, then bring you back to SlyTab.

What is new since the last one:

  - The app now knows whether the receipt reader is available BEFORE you
    take the photo. When it is off, the scan buttons stay where they are but
    are greyed out with the reason, and you can still add the expense by
    hand. No more photographing a receipt into a reader that is down.
  - When the reader is busy with someone else's receipt, your scan is no
    longer refused. The photo is saved, you are shown your place in line and
    roughly how long it will be, and the scan finishes by itself when your
    turn comes. Cancel gives your place up.

Carried over from the update before it, in case you skipped that one:

  - Settle up from either end: tap anyone's balance and record what they
    handed you, including part of it.
  - Lock a trip for settling up, so the numbers hold still while everyone
    pays each other; send one reminder to someone who still owes you.

If anything is wrong, please tell us from Profile -> Report a bug.
